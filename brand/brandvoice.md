# TerraPlan — Brand Voice

> How TerraPlan sounds in words. Use this guide for every page, email, post,
> and proposal.

---

## 1. Reading Level

We write at a **4th-to-5th grade reading level** (Flesch-Kincaid).

This is on purpose. Plain words sound human. Short sentences feel calm. Our
clients are smart people. They are also busy people. They should never have to
work to understand us.

**The rule:** if a 10-year-old would stumble on a word, find a simpler one.

Keep most sentences short. Aim for about 10 words. Use one idea per sentence.
A few longer sentences are fine. They add rhythm. But never let a sentence get
hard to follow.

---

## 2. Our Role: The Guide

This is the heart of our voice.

The client is the hero of the story. They own the land. They have the dream.
The land is theirs.

We are the guide. We have the map. We have walked this path many times.

So we talk about *them* far more than we talk about *us*. We lead with their
goal, not our service list. We say "your land" and "your plan" — not "our
process."

**A quick test:** count the word "you" and the word "we" in any piece of copy.
"You" should win.

A good guide shows two things. First, that we *understand* the client. Second,
that we *know how* to help. Our voice must always carry both.

---

## 3. Voice Pillars

Four traits shape everything we write.

### Calm and confident
We never shout. We never hype. We state what is true and let it stand. A
client should feel that we have done this before — because we have.

### Clear and plain
We use small words. We cut jargon. When a hard term must appear, we explain it
in plain speech right after. Clarity is a form of respect.

### Warm and personal
We sound like a trusted person, not a brochure. We are kind. We are human. We
write the way we would speak to a client across a table.

### Honest and grounded
We tell the truth about the land. We do not promise what the terrain or the
county will not allow. Honest words now save the client pain later.

---

## 4. Tone by Setting

Our voice stays the same. Our tone shifts a little to fit the moment.

| Setting | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Website pages | Warm, clear, and inviting. Lead with the client's dream. |
| Proposals | Calm and precise. Show command of the work. Still plain. |
| Email | Personal and direct. Short. One clear next step. |
| Social posts | Light and human. Share a view, a fact, or a small story. |
| Permitting and agency notes | Exact and formal. Plain, but no loose words. |

## 5. Words We Use — and Skip

### Say this

- "your land," "your plan," "your goal"
- "we handle it," "we guide you," "we take it from here"
- "what's possible," "a clear path," "from first idea to approved permit"
- "survey," "site plan," "grading," "feasibility" — explained simply

### Skip this

- Hype words: "world-class," "best-in-class," "revolutionary," "synergy"
- Cold corporate speak: "leverage," "utilize," "solutions," "deliverables" (in
  client-facing copy)
- Fear or pressure: "act now," "don't miss out," "last chance"
- Empty filler: "very," "really," "in order to," "at the end of the day"

**Swap guide:**

| Instead of | Write |
|------------|-------|
| utilize | use |
| commence | start |
| in order to | to |
| facilitate | help |
| numerous | many |
| terminate | end |
| at this point in time | now |

---

## 6. Sentence Rules

1. Keep most sentences near 10 words.
2. One idea per sentence.
3. Use the active voice. "We handle the county." Not "The county is handled."
4. Start with the client when you can. "You" is a strong first word.
5. Break up long thoughts. Two short lines beat one long one.
6. Read it out loud. If you run out of breath, the sentence is too long.
7. Cut every word that does no work.

## 7. Example Copy

All examples below sit at a 4th-to-5th grade reading level.

### Homepage hero

> **You own the land. We help you see what it can become.**
>
> TerraPlan turns raw land into a clear, approved plan. We handle the survey.
> We handle the design. We handle the county. You stay in charge the whole
> way.
>
> *[ Start with a land review ]*

### Service blurb — survey and terrain

> **First, we learn the land.**
>
> We map your property with care. We use LiDAR to scan the ground. We build
> the contours. We make the ortho images. Now we know the land — its slopes,
> its shape, its limits. Every plan starts here.

### Email — first reply to a new owner

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for reaching out. I would love to hear about your land and what
> you hope to do with it.
>
> Here is how we start. We take a first look at the property. We tell you what
> looks possible. There is no pressure and no cost for that first talk.
>
> Are you free for a short call this week?
>
> Warmly,
> [Name], TerraPlan

### Social post

> Every great build starts with one question: *what could this land become?*
>
> We help land owners answer it. Then we help them make it real — from the
> first survey to the approved permit.
>
> Your land has a story. Let's plan the next chapter.

### Call-to-action lines

- Start with a land review
- See what your land can do
- Let's plan your next step
- Talk to a guide

## 8. The Voice in One Page

- The client is the hero. We are the guide.
- Write "you" more than "we."
- Lead with their dream. Follow with our plan.
- Short sentences. Small words. One idea at a time.
- Calm, clear, warm, and honest — always.
- No hype. No fear. No jargon.
- Read it out loud before it ships.

**If the words feel human and kind, the voice is right.**
